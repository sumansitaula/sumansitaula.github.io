My Story

Finance. Technology.
Conscious Living

My career began in internal audit and compliance, where I built a strong foundation in governance, risk, and financial discipline. Driven by a curiosity for technology and innovation, I soon found my passion in helping businesses improve the way they operate.

Over the past 15+ years, I have worked at the intersection of internal audit, technology, and business transformation, leading ERP implementations, ISO certification initiatives, Quality Management Systems (QMS), Power BI reporting, and digital transformation projects that improve performance and enable sustainable growth.

As a Fellow Chartered Certified Accountant (FCCA) and ACCA tutor, I have also had the privilege of mentoring future finance professionals, many of whom have achieved national and global recognition.

Beyond my corporate work, I explore disciplines that deepen decision-making and personal growth, including advanced numerology and certified residential Vastu consultation. While different in practice, they share a common thread with my professional work: understanding systems, identifying patterns, and helping people make better decisions.

I believe that when financial discipline, technology, and continuous learning come together, they create lasting value for individuals, businesses, and society.
